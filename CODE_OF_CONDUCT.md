# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, appearance, ability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions from new developers and non-developers.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas and behavior, not people.
- Protect private data and never ask another contributor to publish credentials or env files.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private project data, or vulnerability details.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/haechan1103). Do not open a public issue about an individual. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
